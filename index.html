Support & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my BillMint Pro subscription on a new iPhone?

Open BillMint, go to the Reports tab, and tap Restore Purchases (or tap Restore in the top-right corner of the upgrade screen). Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed exclusively by Apple. Open Reports → Manage Subscription inside BillMint, or go to iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions and cancel in one tap at least 24 hours before renewal.

Will I lose access to my past invoices if I cancel Pro?

Never. All invoices, estimates, clients, and receipts are stored locally on your device. You always retain full read, share, and JSON/CSV export access to your existing records.

How do I contact support or report a bug?

Email us directly at baki.glaurung@gmail.com with your iOS version and a short description of what you need help with. We respond directly by email — no login or ticket portal required.
