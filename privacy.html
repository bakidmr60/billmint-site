1. Local-First Privacy by Design

BillMint stores your invoices, estimates, client contacts, price book items, business profile, drawn signatures, job-site photos, time logs, and scanned receipts locally on your iOS device using Apple SwiftData. We do not operate a cloud server that collects, mirrors, tracks, or sells your business or client records.

2. On-Device Processing Only

Receipt text extraction uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Vector PDF generation, QR payment link rendering, and CSV/JSON exports execute entirely on your iPhone without uploading documents or photos to third-party servers.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

BillMint does not collect personal data, does not run third-party advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Subscriptions & Billing

In-app subscriptions (BillMint Pro Annual and BillMint Pro Weekly) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. BillMint never receives, processes, or stores your credit card or Apple Account payment credentials.

5. Device Permissions

Camera: Requested only when you choose to scan a paper receipt or take a job-site photo.
Photo Library: Requested only when you choose to upload your business logo, attach job photos, or import a receipt image.
Notifications (Optional): Requested only if you enable the optional Day-2 Free Trial Reminder toggle.

6. Full User Data Ownership

You control your records at all times. You may view, export (PDF, CSV ledger, or full JSON backup), or permanently delete any document or delete the app at any time — including after canceling a subscription.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us directly at baki.glaurung@gmail.com.
